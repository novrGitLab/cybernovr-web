## SECTION 2: Staff Knowledge Assessment (20 Technical Questions)

### Q1. What script/command in MSF console is used to find vulnerabilities and gain access to a system by exploiting weak points?
- A) db_nmap
- B) search
- C) db_autopwn ✅
- D) exploit

**Answer:** db_autopwn

**Explanation:** db_autopwn is a Metasploit console command that automatically exploits vulnerabilities found during scanning.

---

### Q2. What is the best approach to apply patching?
- A) Manual Patching
- B) Automatic Patching
- C) Mixture ✅
- D) Other

**Answer:** Mixture

**Explanation:** A mixture of manual and automatic patching is considered best practice, allowing for both control and efficiency.

---

### Q3. A system was infected with ransomware and left powered on. What's the best tool to begin memory analysis in live forensics?
- A) Autopsy
- B) FTK Imager
- C) Redline ✅
- D) Wireshark

**Answer:** Redline

**Explanation:** Redline is specifically designed for memory analysis in live forensics situations.

---

### Q4. RAID 6 uses what to provide fault tolerance?
- A) Parity and striping
- B) Dual parity ✅
- C) Mirroring
- D) Encryption

**Answer:** Dual parity

**Explanation:** RAID 6 uses dual parity to provide fault tolerance against two simultaneous drive failures.

---

### Q5. Where should AI be prioritized most in network security?
- A) Network traffic analysis
- B) Threat detection & response ✅
- C) Policy enforcement
- D) Identity & access control

**Answer:** Threat detection & response

**Explanation:** AI is most effective in threat detection and response, where it can analyze patterns and respond in real-time.

---

### Q6. Where did the term "hacker" originate?
- A) MIT ✅
- B) New York University
- C) Harvard University
- D) Bell's Lab

**Answer:** MIT

**Explanation:** The term originated at MIT in the 1960s among members of the Tech Model Railroad Club.

---

### Q7. Would you allow your staff to use AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Google Veo in your organisation?
- A) Yes
- B) No
- C) Maybe ✅

**Answer:** Maybe

**Explanation:** A cautious "maybe" with proper guidelines is often the best approach for AI tool usage in organizations.

---

### Q8. Which layer of the OSI model is responsible for logical addressing?
- A) Network Layer ✅
- B) Data Link
- C) Transport Layer
- D) Application Layer

**Answer:** Network Layer

**Explanation:** The Network Layer (Layer 3) handles logical addressing and routing.

---

### Q9. Which protocol is considered secure for web communication?
- A) HTTP
- B) FTP
- C) HTTPS ✅
- D) Telnet

**Answer:** HTTPS

**Explanation:** HTTPS (Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure) is the secure version of HTTP that uses SSL/TLS encryption to protect data during transmission.

---

### Q10. What is the most common form of cyberattack on networks?
- A) Brute force attack
- B) Man-in-the-middle attack
- C) Phishing ✅
- D) SQL injection

**Answer:** Phishing

**Explanation:** Phishing accounts for over 90% of cyberattacks, targeting human vulnerabilities rather than technical weaknesses.

---

### Q11. Which of these is a sign of malware infection?
- A) Faster performance
- B) Frequent crashes ✅
- C) New features
- D) More storage

**Answer:** Frequent crashes

**Explanation:** Frequent crashes or unusual system behavior are common indicators of malware infection.

---

### Q12. Why does port 443 matter for SSL/TLS secure web traffic?
- A) Encrypted connection ✅
- B) Cert validation
- C) No fallback
- D) Bypass firewall

**Answer:** Encrypted connection

**Explanation:** Port 443 is the standard port used for HTTPS traffic, providing encrypted communication between web browsers and servers.

---

### Q13. Which of these is used to encrypt emails?
- A) SSL
- B) TLS
- C) PGP ✅
- D) HTTP

**Answer:** PGP

**Explanation:** PGP (Pretty Good Privacy) is specifically designed for encrypting email communications.

---

### Q14. The act of disguising as someone else online is called:
- A) Encryption
- B) Impersonation
- C) Spoofing ✅
- D) Harvesting

**Answer:** Spoofing

**Explanation:** Spoofing involves falsifying data to appear as another user, device, or system.

---

### Q15. What do you think is the most damaging impact of a cyberattack on a school/college?
- A) Student data leakage ✅
- B) Financial extortion (ransom)
- C) Loss of academic records
- D) Reputation damage

**Answer:** Student data leakage

**Explanation:** Student data leakage can lead to identity theft, privacy violations, and long-term consequences for affected individuals.

---

### Q16. Which of the following is not an email related hacking tool?
- A) Mail Password
- B) Mail PassView
- C) Sendinc ✅
- D) Email Finder Pro

**Answer:** Sendinc

**Explanation:** Sendinc is a legitimate email service, unlike the others which are associated with extracting or discovering email credentials.

---

### Q17. Which one of these is a symmetric encryption algorithm?
- A) DSA
- B) ECC
- C) DES ✅
- D) RSA

**Answer:** DES

**Explanation:** DES (Data Encryption Standard) uses the same key for encryption and decryption, making it a symmetric algorithm.

---

### Q18. Which of the following encryption methods is considered the most secure for data-at-rest?
- A) AES-128
- B) RSA
- C) AES-256 ✅
- D) Triple DES

**Answer:** AES-256

**Explanation:** AES-256 is widely regarded as the strongest commonly used encryption standard for protecting stored data.

---

### Q19. What is the biggest concern for an organisation after the mega-breach?
- A) Business email compromise
- B) Personal account hacks
- C) Dark web exposure ✅
- D) Credential phishing

**Answer:** Dark web exposure

**Explanation:** Dark web exposure of sensitive data often has the longest-lasting consequences.

---

### Q20. After reading about the 16B password leak, what's your next step?
- A) Change all my passwords
- B) Enable MFA everywhere ✅
- C) Check if I'm affected
- D) I'm not sure what to do

**Answer:** Enable MFA everywhere

**Explanation:** Enabling Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) provides the most immediate security improvement after a breach.
